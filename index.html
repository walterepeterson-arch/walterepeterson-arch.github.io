About Me

Personal Background

Originally from Kolkata, India, I value meaningful relationships, personal growth and the importance of maintaining a positive outlook towards life. I consider myself an approachable, cheerful and optimistic individual.

Faith & Values

Christian faith plays a central role in my life and shapes my values and outlook. I believe in God's guidance and grace, and strive to uphold principles of faith, integrity, compassion, gratitude and respect in my everyday life.

Professional Career

I am associated with the airline industry in the capacity of a Subject Matter Expert (SME). My professional journey reflects my interest in aviation, industry knowledge, operational excellence and continuous learning.

Interests & Lifestyle

My interests include travelling, exploring different cultures, aviation, photography, music and discovering new experiences. I particularly value opportunities to connect with people, broaden my perspectives and create lasting memories.

Personal Philosophy

I believe in living with purpose, maintaining a positive attitude, embracing opportunities for growth and appreciating the experiences that shape our lives.
